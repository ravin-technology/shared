# Flags in the sand: how to set it up

**TL;DR:** Attach `flags-in-the-sand-SKILL.md` in a chat with your main Grok bot and say "save this as a skill." After that, tell any of your bots to "plant a flag" whenever you think of something you don't want to forget, and ask "any flags?" when you're ready to review them.

## What it is
A flag is a quick, untimed note-to-self. You drop one in the middle of a conversation so you don't lose the thought or have to switch topics. Flags are tagged by nouns (people, places, projects, things), so you can pull up everything about a topic later. There are two kinds:

- **Action** flags are things to do or review, usually with someone. For example: "Review the server setup with Kewl."
- **Remember** flags are facts to recall later. For example: "The hot water heater wrenches are in a box in the camper."

Your daily reports (if you have any) can show a count of action flags by who they're with, like "Flags: Kewl 1, Tim 2." Remember flags only come up when you ask.

## Setup (about 2 minutes)
1. Open a chat with your main Grok bot (the one you'd want keeping track of things).
2. Attach `flags-in-the-sand-SKILL.md` and say: **"Save this as a skill."**
3. Tell that bot: **"You're the keeper of the flags. Let my other bots know they can add flags too."** Your bots share one computer, so they all read and write the same flags list.
4. Optional: if you get a daily summary or report from a bot, say **"Add a line to my report counting open action flags by who they're with."**

## Everyday use
Say these to any of your bots:

| You want to... | Say something like |
|---|---|
| Plant a flag | "Add a flag: Jace's helmet is on the computer desk." |
| Flag something to do with someone | "Flag this for Kewl: do we still need the Docker repos?" |
| Find something | "Where's Jace's helmet?" |
| See everything on a topic | "Show me all flags about Morse code." |
| Review with a bot | "Any flags with you?" |
| Review everything | "Any flags we should review?" |
| Close one out | "F002 is done, we dropped the Docker repos." |

## Notes: extra context on a flag
When a bot looks something up or you explain the background, it can attach notes to the flag (research, links, options). The flag stays one line in the list, and the details go in a notes file named after the flag's number. Flags with notes are marked **[notes]**. When you review that flag later, everything's already there, so nothing has to be researched again. Notes stay with a flag even after it's resolved.

## Tips
- Don't ask the bot to confirm anything. Just say "flag it" and keep talking.
- If a flag has a time ("remind me Friday"), it's a reminder, not a flag. Ask your bot for a routine instead.
- Never put passwords or codes in a flag. Flag where they're kept instead.
